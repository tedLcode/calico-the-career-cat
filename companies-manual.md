# Manual-check companies

These don't publish a machine-readable job feed the pipeline can use (Oracle HCM, SuccessFactors, custom portals, or LinkedIn-only), so check them by hand — weekly is enough. Chennai-relevant ones first.

| Company | Where to check | Why it's worth it |
|---|---|---|
